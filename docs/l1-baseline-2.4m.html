Curriculum works. Restricting to 1 house + 1 goal gives the world model enough repeated exposure (7,499 episodes in one scene) to learn useful dynamics. All-scenes baseline (2.36% SR) failed because 33 episodes/house was too sparse.

Learning Curves

SR rises rapidly in first 200K steps, plateaus at 55–75%. SPL follows a similar trajectory, reaching ~0.5.

World Model Losses

Val dyn loss increases (17 → 42)

The model overfits to training trajectories. It memorizes dynamics from 7,499 training starts but cannot generalize to validation starts it hasn’t seen. Train/val gap widens throughout training.

Reward loss converges fast

Train reward loss drops from 5.4 to ~0.03 within 100K steps. The world model quickly learns the geodesic-delta reward signal in this single-house setting.

Policy Loss & KL Divergence

Policy loss is negative — expected

Policy loss = -E[returns]. Negative means positive expected returns. The value of -0.03 is small because DreamerV3 normalizes returns with percentile-based scaling.

KL divergence rises (2.9 → 6.0)

The posterior learns faster than the prior. The encoder extracts richer information from observations than the prior (action + previous state) can predict. A known DreamerV3 behavior, not a failure.

Episode Reward & Length

Agent gets efficient: Episode length drops from 500 (max timeout) to ~250 steps. Reward rises from ~0 to ~10 (success bonus). The agent learns purposeful navigation, not random wandering.

Key Findings & Next Steps

What worked

  • L1 curriculum enables learning (67% SR vs 2.36% all-scenes)
  • World model learns scene dynamics
  • Policy discovers navigation behavior
  • Agent efficiency improves (500 → 250 steps)

Concerns

  • Val dyn loss 7x higher than train (overfitting)
  • SR plateaus at ~67%, no improvement after 500K
  • KL divergence rising throughout
  • No hard episodes (>7m) — untested on difficult nav

Next: Add step penalty (-0.01) to improve SPL → L2 curriculum (1 house, 6 goals) → investigate regularization for overfitting → L3/L4 multi-house curricula with hard episodes
