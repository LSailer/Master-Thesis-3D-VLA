Findings

3.84% SR is the random floor

Higher than the 2.36% all-scenes baseline because L1 uses a single house with many chair instances, increasing proximity probability.

Step penalty dominates reward

-0.01 × 489 steps = -4.89 penalty. Random geodesic deltas add ~+0.49. A trained agent that moves purposefully will have much higher deltas and fewer steps.

No partial progress

Bimodal reward distribution: either full failure (-5) or full success (+10). No middle ground, confirming random actions don't produce sustained approach behavior.

Lucky spawns drive success

32 successful episodes terminate as early as 30 steps. These are episodes where the agent spawns near a chair and stumbles into it by chance.
