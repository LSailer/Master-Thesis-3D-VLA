TL;DR — core reading set

If you read only five sources to ground a DreamerV3 + 3D-features thesis in MBRL:

  1. Moerland et al. (2023) — the unifying MBRL survey/monograph; primary framework citation.
  2. Plaat (2022), Chapter 5 — best textbook chapter explicitly covering Dreamer / PlaNet.
  3. Sutton & Barto (2018), Chapter 8 — canonical reference for the planning-vs-learning distinction and Dyna.
  4. Hafner (2024) — the Dreamer PhD thesis; canonical for every architectural choice in your code.
  5. Deisenroth (2010) — PILCO; historical anchor for the data-efficiency argument for MBRL.

Conceptually: MBRL learns a transition model $\hat{p}(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)$ and a reward model $\hat{r}(s_t,a_t)$, then either plans (MPC, MCTS) or trains a policy on imagined rollouts $\hat{\tau} = (\hat{s}_0,a_0,\hat{s}_1,\ldots)$. DreamerV3 sits in the latter camp: it learns a latent RSSM and trains an actor–critic on imagined latent trajectories.

Part 1 — Books on Model-Based Reinforcement Learning

Ordered by relevance to world-model agents. Each entry notes whether MBRL is the main topic or a chapter, the level of treatment, and a one-line assessment.

Core — must-read for an MBRL thesis

1. Moerland, Broekens, Plaat & Jonker — Model-based Reinforcement Learning: A Survey

Foundations & Trends in ML 16(1), now publishers, 2023 · ~118 pp. · MBRL: entire book

  • Structure: §3 categories of MBRL · §4 dynamics-model learning (stochasticity, uncertainty, partial observability, multi-step prediction, abstraction) · §5 integration of planning and learning · §6 implicit MBRL (value-equivalent models, learning-to-plan) · §7 benefits (data efficiency, exploration, transfer, safety).
  • Methods: Dyna, PETS, World Models, PlaNet, Dreamer, MuZero, AlphaZero, MCTS, value-equivalent models, MPC, latent-space planning.
  • Level: graduate / research monograph.

The single most useful reference for your thesis. The §4–§6 taxonomy maps directly onto design choices for a DreamerV3-style agent (latent dynamics, imagination horizon, planning budget). arXiv:2006.16712

2. Plaat — Deep Reinforcement Learning

Springer, 2022 · MBRL: Chapter 5

  • Chapter 5 "Model-Based Reinforcement Learning" sits between policy-based RL (Ch. 4) and self-play (Ch. 6). Covers tabular models, learned-NN dynamics, planning vs. learning, latent models incl. World Models / PlaNet / Dreamer, and links forward to MuZero in Ch. 6.
  • Level: graduate textbook, accessible.

Best textbook-style introduction with explicit Dreamer/PlaNet coverage at thesis-intro level. Pair with the Moerland survey. arXiv:2201.02135

3. Sutton & Barto — Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd ed.

MIT Press, 2018 · MBRL: Chapter 8

  • Chapter 8 "Planning and Learning with Tabular Methods": 8.1 Models & Planning · 8.2 Dyna · 8.3 When the Model Is Wrong · 8.4 Prioritized Sweeping · 8.5 Expected vs. Sample Updates · 8.6 Trajectory Sampling · 8.7 Real-time DP · 8.8 Planning at Decision Time · 8.9 Heuristic Search · 8.10 Rollout · 8.11 MCTS.
  • Methods: Dyna-Q, prioritized sweeping, MCTS, rollout.
  • Level: introductory / graduate.

Canonical citation for the planning-vs-learning distinction and Dyna-style background updates that DreamerV3 generalizes. Use for foundations; doesn't cover deep latent world models. Book site

Optimal-control / MPC framings

4. Bertsekas — Reinforcement Learning and Optimal Control

Athena Scientific, 2019 · MBRL: book-wide (ADP viewpoint)

  • Whole book builds on the model-based / approximate-DP viewpoint. Heavy on one-step / multistep lookahead, parametric approximation, rollout (§4.4ff), MCTS, and the equivalence between MPC and rollout.
  • Level: graduate / research, mathematically demanding.

Useful as the optimal-control bridge: frames imagination horizon as approximate-in-value-space lookahead, and Dreamer's actor–critic over imagined trajectories as actor–critic-with-rollout. Not where you'll find Dreamer itself. Book site

5. Bertsekas — Lessons from AlphaZero for Optimal, Model Predictive, and Adaptive Control

Athena Scientific, 2022 · MBRL: entire book

  • Unifies AlphaZero (off-line training + on-line rollout) with MPC and adaptive control via Newton's method.
  • Level: research monograph.

Excellent for a "Discussion / Related Work" section linking learned-model agents (MuZero/Dreamer) to classical MPC. arXiv:2108.10315

6. Powell — Reinforcement Learning and Stochastic Optimization

Wiley, 2022 · ~1100 pp. · MBRL: Part on Lookahead Policies

  • Chapter 19 "Direct Lookahead Policies": deterministic lookahead = MPC; stochastic lookahead; six classes of approximation; "policy-within-a-policy" structure. Four-class taxonomy (PFA, CFA, VFA, DLA).
  • Level: graduate, encyclopedic.

Useful vocabulary if your thesis addresses OR/control audiences. Cite for the MPC = direct-lookahead identity. CASTLE Lab

Surveys, theory, and edited volumes

7. François-Lavet et al. — An Introduction to Deep Reinforcement Learning

FnT in ML 11(3-4), 2018 · ~150 pp. · MBRL: Chapter 6

  • Chapter 6: 6.1 Pure model-based methods · 6.2 Integrating model-free and model-based methods.
  • Predates DreamerV2/V3 but covers World Models, I2A, Dyna-style hybrids.

Compact and citable; supersede with Moerland (2023) for current methods. arXiv:1811.12560

8. Szepesvári — Algorithms for Reinforcement Learning

Morgan & Claypool, 2010 (Springer reissue 2022) · ~100 pp.

  • Touches R-MAX, E³, certainty equivalence in the Control chapter. No deep MBRL.
  • Level: graduate, theoretical.

Cite for PAC-MDP / sample-complexity arguments behind the "models are sample-efficient" claim. Book site

9. Wiering & van Otterlo (eds.) — Reinforcement Learning: State-of-the-Art

Springer ALO 12, 2012

  • Hester & Stone chapter "Learning and Using Models": cleanest tabular-MBRL chapter overview (pre-deep-learning).

Historical anchor for the model-learning-then-planning paradigm; dated for methods. Volume

Practitioner / introductory (limited MBRL)

10. Lapan — Deep RL Hands-On, 3rd ed.

Packt, Nov 2024

No dedicated MBRL chapter, but Chapter 20 "MuZero". PyTorch code reference only. Skip unless you need code. GitHub

11. Morales — Grokking Deep RL

Manning, 2020

Deep-MBRL essentially absent. Friendly model-free primer; skip for MBRL. Publisher

No mainstream textbook to date has a full Dreamer / world-models chapter beyond Plaat (2022). For state-of-the-art coverage, use Moerland (2023) plus the primary DreamerV3 Nature 2025 paper.

Part 2 — Master's & PhD theses on Model-Based RL

Tier 1 — Direct lineage (Dreamer / World Models / PILCO)

PhD1. Danijar Hafner — Embodied Intelligence Through World Models

University of Toronto (ECE), 2024 · Supervisor: Jimmy Ba

  • Summary: The canonical Dreamer thesis. Develops PlaNet → DreamerV1/V2/V3, the Recurrent State-Space Model (RSSM), symlog scaling, and DreamerV3's single-hyperparameter generalization across 150+ tasks.
  • Methods: PlaNet, DreamerV1/V2/V3, RSSM, latent imagination.

The foundational reference for your codebase. Cite for every architectural choice in DreamerV3. UToronto Scholaris

PhD2. Marc Peter Deisenroth — Efficient Reinforcement Learning using Gaussian Processes

KIT, 2010 · Co-advisor: Carl Edward Rasmussen (Cambridge)

  • Summary: Introduces PILCO — probabilistic, data-efficient model-based policy search using GP dynamics models with analytic gradients via moment matching. Cart-pole swing-up solved in 17.5 s of physical interaction.
  • Methods: PILCO, GP dynamics, probabilistic policy search, model-bias mitigation.

Historical antecedent of modern world-model RL. Best citation for the "data efficiency of MBRL" framing in your related-work chapter. PDF

PhD3. Anusha Nagabandi — Model-based Deep RL for Robotic Systems

UC Berkeley EECS, August 2020 · Supervisor: Sergey Levine

  • Summary: Deep-MBRL framework for legged locomotion (6-DoF) and dexterous manipulation (Shadow Hand, 24-DoF). Neural-network dynamics + MPC, then meta-learning for online adaptation (MoLe, PDDM).
  • Methods: NN dynamics, MPC/CEM, meta-learned dynamics, model-free fine-tuning.

Best single source on how deep dynamics models transfer to real robots. EECS Tech Report

PhD4. Thanard Kurutach — Learning, Planning, and Acting with Models

UC Berkeley EECS, May 2021 · Supervisors: Stuart Russell, Pieter Abbeel

  • Summary: Model-ensemble TRPO (ME-TRPO), Causal InfoGAN for plannable representations, and visual planning for manipulation.
  • Methods: Ensemble dynamics, latent plannable representations, visual planning.

Relevant to plugging VGGT/UNITE 3D features into a Dreamer-style world model. EECS Tech Report

Tier 2 — Deep RL foundations around world models

PhD5. Chelsea Finn — Learning to Learn with Gradients

UC Berkeley EECS, August 2018 · Supervisors: Pieter Abbeel, Sergey Levine · ACM Doctoral Dissertation Award 2018

  • Introduces MAML; the visual-foresight chapter learns video-prediction world models for robot pushing/manipulation and plans with sampling-based MPC in pixel space.

Early example of visual world models for robotics — predecessor to Dreamer's latent approach. PDF

PhD6. Pieter Abbeel — Apprenticeship Learning and RL with Application to Robotic Control

Stanford CS, August 2008 · Supervisor: Andrew Y. Ng

  • Early model-based RL on real helicopters and cars using learned dynamics plus inverse-RL-derived rewards.

Foundational "MBRL works on real hardware" reference. PDF

PhD7. Sergey Levine — Motor Skill Learning with Local Trajectory Methods

Stanford CS, 2014 · Supervisor: Vladlen Koltun

  • Guided policy search via local linear-Gaussian dynamics — precursor to deep MBRL.

Roots of the Berkeley MBRL school. URL unverified — check Stanford SearchWorks

PhD8. Volodymyr Mnih — Machine Learning for Aerial Image Labeling

University of Toronto CS, 2013 · Supervisor: Geoffrey Hinton

  • The thesis itself is on aerial image labeling — Mnih is the DQN architect; cite for context on the deep-RL → world-model transition.

Background for the "from model-free to model-based deep RL" narrative. PDF

Tier 3 — Search-based MBRL (AlphaZero / MuZero family)

PhD9. Ivo Danihelka — Planning and Policy Improvement

UCL (with DeepMind), 2023 · Supervisor: David Silver

  • Develops Gumbel AlphaZero / Gumbel MuZero, advancing policy-improvement operators inside learned-model search.

Companion reference for the discrete-action / search-based branch of MBRL — contrast with Dreamer. PDF

PhD10. UCL — Learning to Search in Reinforcement Learning

UCL Discovery, DeepMind-affiliated · likely Thomas Anthony (Expert Iteration)

  • Search-based RL combining deep networks with MCTS; develops Expert Iteration.

Author/title not fully verified — confirm via the repository page before citing. UCL record

PhD11. Vincent François-Lavet — Contributions to Deep RL and Applications in Smartgrids

University of Liège, 2017 · Supervisor: Damien Ernst

  • Includes CRAR (Combined RL via Abstract Representations) — jointly learns model-free Q-values and a model-based latent dynamics model. Early hybrid world-model approach.

Clean exposition of latent-space planning vs. pixel reconstruction trade-offs — directly applicable to how VGGT/UNITE features should feed an RSSM. PDF

Tier 4 — Recent (2021–2025): navigation, embodied agents, 3D perception

PhD12. Lisa Lee — Learning Embodied Agents with Scalably-Supervised RL

CMU MLD, 2021 · Supervisors: Ruslan Salakhutdinov, Eric Xing

  • Trains embodied agents in 3D environments from first-person RGB with language-specified goals; scalable supervision for navigation and instruction-following.
  • Mostly model-free with auxiliary self-supervision, but includes latent world-model components.

Direct precedent for the ObjectNav setting in HM3D-style 3D scenes. PDF

PhD13. Cherie Ho — Flexible Perception for High-Performance Robot Navigation

CMU Robotics Institute, 2025 · Supervisor: Sebastian Scherer

  • Perception architectures for robot navigation under varying sensing modalities; learned representations for downstream planning.

Practical reference for how 3D perception integrates with downstream navigation policies — analogous to plugging VGGT/UNITE into ObjectNav. PDF

MSc14. Wenli Xiao — Foundation Control Model for General Embodied Intelligence

CMU Robotics Institute (MSR), 2025

  • Foundation models for embodied control — world-model-adjacent.

Useful template for an MSc thesis bridging large pretrained models and control. PDF

PhD15. Stanford — Manipulation and Reasoning Methods for Embodied Object Navigation

Stanford University

  • Combines manipulation and high-level reasoning for embodied object navigation — adjacent to your HM3D ObjectNav scope.

Author name not extracted from search snippet — verify on the Stanford landing page. PDF

What is not a thesis

  • Ha & Schmidhuber, "World Models" (2018) is a NeurIPS paper, not a thesis. Cite the paper directly: arXiv:1803.10122.
  • DayDreamer (Wu, Escontrela, Hafner, Goldberg, Abbeel, CoRL 2022) — paper, not a thesis. arXiv:2206.14176
  • Sebastian Thrun's PhD (1995, Bonn) is on explanation-based neural-network learning, not directly MBRL.

Mental model

Across all of this material, MBRL boils down to choosing four design axes:

  1. Model representation: tabular · GP · NN ensemble · latent RSSM · value-equivalent.
  2. How the model is used: background updates (Dyna) · imagination rollouts · MPC planning · MCTS search · value-equivalent gradient through the model.
  3. Where the loss lives: reconstruction in pixel space (PlaNet, World Models) · latent-only / value-equivalent (DreamerV3 partially, MuZero) · model-free auxiliary.
  4. Uncertainty: single model · ensemble · probabilistic (GP, Bayesian NN) · ignored.

DreamerV3's slot is: latent RSSM, imagination rollouts, mostly reconstruction-based loss with symlog scaling, single model. Your 3D-features contribution lives entirely in axis 1 (replacing pixel-conv encoders with VGGT/UNITE features), so the literature axis you'll lean on hardest is "model representation" — i.e. Hafner's RSSM design choices, Kurutach's representation-for-planning thread, and the visual-foresight line in Finn.
